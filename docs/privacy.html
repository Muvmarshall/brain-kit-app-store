Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · App: Brain Kit Practice (free v1) · Publisher: Mike Marshall

Short version for parents: Practice progress stays on the device. This free version has no third-party ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no required child accounts. Questions: miketmarshall94@gmail.com.

Who this app is for

Brain Kit Practice is a K–12 reading and math practice app for families. A parent or guardian should set up the household, parent PIN, and any outbound link opens (parental gate).

Information handled on the device

In free v1, Brain Kit is designed as a local-first Capacitor app. Typical on-device data may include:

  • Child first name and grade for profiles you create
  • Parent email you enter for the household (if used)
  • Practice progress, session history, goals, and settings (stored locally on the device)
  • A parent PIN used only on-device for Parent HQ / parental gates — we do not receive your PIN

We do not require children to create accounts, provide last names, precise location, photos, contacts, or behavioral advertising identifiers to practice.

What we do not do in free v1

  • No third-party advertising networks
  • No third-party analytics / profiling SDKs in this version
  • No social feed or social login for children
  • No sale of children’s personal information
  • No In-App Purchases or subscriptions in Brain Kit Practice 1.0 (free)

Servers and optional features

Core diagnostic and practice in free v1 are intended to work with progress kept on-device. We do not operate a required child-account backend for this free release. If a later version loads content from a hosted HTTPS URL or syncs household data to a family backend, we will update this policy and App Store privacy labels before relying on that path.

Apple may process technical diagnostics (for example aggregate crash reports) as part of App Store distribution. That is Apple’s processing, not a third-party ad or analytics SDK we embed for profiling kids.

Children’s privacy

Brain Kit Practice is directed in part to children. We limit collection to what is needed to run local practice and parent controls. This page describes our practices honestly; it is not a claim of third-party COPPA certification or a seal from any auditor.

Parents can review, export, or delete a child’s profile from Parent HQ on the device where the data lives, and can email us with questions.

Parental controls

  • Parent PIN for Parent HQ and purchase / outbound gates
  • External links open only after a parental gate
  • Consent before student profiles are created

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change, and the new version will be posted at this Privacy URL.
