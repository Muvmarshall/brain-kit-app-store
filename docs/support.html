What Brain Kit Practice is

A local-first K–12 practice app: diagnostics and practice on the device, parental gate before external links, no third-party ads in free v1, and no In-App Purchases in Practice 1.0.

Common help

  • Parent PIN / Parent HQ — Open Parent tools after the parental gate. Use the PIN you set on this device.
  • Forgot PIN — PIN is stored only on the device. Reinstalling or clearing app data resets local data (including PIN and progress). Email us if you need guidance before wiping data.
  • Progress missing — Progress is on-device. A new device, reinstall, or cleared storage will not carry local progress unless you exported it first.
  • External links — Privacy, Terms, and help links require a grown-up gate before opening Safari.
  • Purchases — Free v1 has no IAP or subscriptions. Do not look for Complete / Essentials prices in this build.
